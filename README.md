## 🌱 Featured project: Habit4U

A habit tracker that feels like a game. Build routines, earn XP, level up, keep your streak alive, and look after a pixel pet that grows with you.

- **Routines & to-dos:** daily, weekly and monthly repeats, partial progress, reminders with *Mark Done* / *Snooze* right from the notification
- **Game layer:** XP, levels, streaks with freezes, unlockable pet food and outfits
- **Pet care:** feed, wash and play with your pet, which reacts and gets muddy when neglected
- **Installable app (PWA):** works like a native app on iPhone and Android, with push notifications
- **Three languages:** English, German and Persian (right-to-left)
- **Apple-style design:** light, dark and automatic appearance, accent colours, first-visit tutorials

**Stack:** React 19 · Vite · TypeScript · TanStack Query · NestJS · TypeORM · PostgreSQL · Web Push · Playwright · Vitest

🔗 [habit4u.vercel.app](https://habit4u.vercel.app). Currently in private beta, so access is by invite.
